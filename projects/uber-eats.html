The problem

Marketplace metrics need operational context.

A food-delivery marketplace needs more than a revenue chart. Teams need to see demand by hour, cancellations by cause, zone performance, courier activity and restaurants that may need attention. This portfolio project simulated those operational questions for Sri Lanka using synthetic data rather than real Uber Eats records.

Key decisions

Why I built it this way.

01

Separate generation and analysis

A modular generate → analyze → visualize workflow makes the simulated inputs and transformations clear.

02

Design by business question

Five pages group marketplace, pricing, restaurants, driver operations and churn rather than mixing every chart on one screen.

03

Use a risk score for outreach

A Random Forest model scores restaurant partners so a partner team can prioritize investigation, not treat a score as confirmed future churn.

Results

Findings in the simulated marketplace.

Scope and measurement matter as much as the headline numbers.

01

~82% conversion

Platform-wide conversion in the synthetic order data; the remaining fallout is the gap worth investigating.

02

~45% of demand

Orders between 12–14h and 19–21h account for roughly 45% of simulated daily orders.

03

~15% high risk

The churn model marks about 15% of simulated restaurant partners as high-risk signals.

Measurement: These are descriptive results from generated 2024 marketplace data: 70,000+ orders, 200 restaurants and 300 couriers. The churn target is derived from low volume and low conversion; held-out predictive accuracy is not reported here. The elasticity coefficient is approximately −0.5 in this simulated analysis.

What broke

What I learned while building it.

01

Synthetic data limits

Because the marketplace is simulated, pricing and churn findings demonstrate the method and the modelling approach, not real Sri Lankan market behaviour.

02

Model target design

The churn label is based on low order volume and conversion, so the risk score is a demonstration of prioritization rather than externally validated churn prediction.

03

Synthetic operating context

Data generation enables reproducible scenarios, but the resulting relationships may not represent a live marketplace.

Limitations

What this project does not claim.

All marketplace records are synthetic. The model target is a constructed churn label, and no held-out model accuracy or causal pricing estimate is claimed. Findings are portfolio demonstrations and should not be described as real Uber Eats Sri Lanka performance.
