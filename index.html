How I work

Four habits from production.

Working on live banking databases taught me to trust data only after checking it.

1

Understand the source

Where the data comes from, who depends on it, and what a bad day looks like.

2

Validate early

Checks at each hop, so problems are caught where they start.

3

Build and automate

Containerised, repeatable, and easy to rerun after a failure.

4

Explain the result

Clear numbers, stated limits, and notes the next person can follow.
